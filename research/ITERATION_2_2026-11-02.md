# Trading Quant — iteration 2, 2 Nov 2026

## Decision

No strategy qualifies for paper trading. This iteration tested execution granularity, 5m continuation and reversion, funding carry, and a cross-sectional long/short portfolio under the same risk budget. The best-looking filtered breakout slice fails chronological validation and is dominated by one trade. The last 24 weeks have already been examined across multiple hypotheses, so they are a screened holdout, not untouched OOS.

No live orders were submitted. Continue research with a newly frozen hypothesis; do not deploy the current candidates.

## Data and method

- Downloaded Kraken Futures trade OHLCV at 5m for BTC, ETH, SOL, and XRP from 2 Nov 2023 to 2 Nov 2026: 315,648 bars per contract, no missing 5m intervals, identical timestamps across assets. The four files and SHA-256 manifests are in `data/raw/`.
- The candle audit also passes for the existing 1h, 4h, and 1d series. Historical funding coverage is 26,308/26,316 hourly points (99.9696%) per asset; eight gaps remain missing. No missing funding rate was filled with zero in the data.
- Periods are chronological and independently initialized at $900: train 2 Nov 2023–30 Oct 2025; WF1 30 Oct 2025–1 Feb 2026; WF2 1 Feb–22 May 2026; screened final 22 May–2 Nov 2026. The final interval was reused across several experiments and is exposed to selection bias.
- Every run charges 5 bp taker fee per side, plus 3 bp/side base spread and slippage or 7.5 bp/side stress friction. Funding is applied hourly. Stops use stop-first same-bar resolution; through-stop gaps fill at the bar open. Since Kraken chart candles are trade-price OHLCV, the replay cannot reproduce mark-price liquidation, queue position, partial fills, latency, or market impact.
- The tested one-position portfolios size to 0.25% total initial stop risk, use a 0.75× equity notional cap and enforce daily -1%, weekly -3%, and permanent peak drawdown -6% stops. Maximum leverage was not increased. Drawdown can overshoot slightly on adverse candle extremes and modeled exit friction.
- Funding filter unit correction: `0.00002` means 0.2 bp/hour, not 2 bp/hour. The value was used consistently in calculations; the label in earlier notes was corrected.

The volume-retest signal requires a confirmed 1h breakout/retest in the 4h EMA50/EMA200 trend direction and retest-candle volume below the prior breakout candle. Its screened final average adverse-mark return is +0.304% per week base and +0.249% stress; medians are +0.063% and +0.034%, positive-week share 54.2% and 50.0%, and worst weeks -0.569% and -0.797%. Four-week circular block bootstrap intervals for the mean are +0.047% to +0.617% base and +0.014% to +0.523% stress (24 weekly observations; 5,000 resamples). Those intervals are descriptive and unadjusted for trying multiple variants.

That apparent positive slice fails the validation and concentration requirements: train and both WFs are negative at base and stress costs; only 55/56 trades occur in the final interval; one trade generates 55.9%/62.7% of total net profit, and one week generates 57.8%/64.8%. It is rejected.

The funding carry screen required a 24-hour cross-asset mean funding spread of at least 0.00015/hour (1.5 bp/hour), chosen to exceed the estimated one-day round-trip base cost for two equal-notional legs. Across 1,089 complete UTC days, the median spread was 0.123 bp/hour, the 95th percentile 0.322 bp/hour, and only two days exceeded the cost-derived threshold. No trade was taken; this market set did not offer the modeled carry opportunity often enough.

The 5m Donchian continuation lost the -6% capital stop in every window. VWAP reversion also lost in all windows. The combined regime portfolio did not diversify the components: it lost 4.42–4.95% in training and both WFs and 5.32–5.66% in the screened final interval, approaching the drawdown kill in all four windows. Cross-sectional momentum generated more than 200 screened-final trades but was consistently negative; transaction frequency did not create an edge. The previously tested cross-asset trend and exit variants also failed WF2, regardless of their positive screened-final returns.
